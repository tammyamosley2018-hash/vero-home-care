Who we serve

Care designed around
your journey.

Home care can help when everyday routines become harder to manage alone. Vero supports older adults who want to age in place, adults who need assistance with daily activities, and families who need a trusted helping hand at home.

We also support people returning home after a hospital stay, individuals experiencing changes in mobility or memory, and family caregivers who need time to rest and recharge.

Meet the founder

Markeisha
Eaves, RN

“Caring for people has always been my purpose.”

Markeisha Eaves has served as a Registered Nurse for more than 17 years. Her experience spans PACU, ICU, Medical-Surgical, Telemetry, Cardiac Catheterization, Pre-Operative Services, Emergency Department support, and other areas of patient care.

For the past 10 years, she has also worked as a travel nurse across more than 30 hospitals, including Level I trauma facilities. That experience—and her belief that people deserve dignity, respect, and compassion in their own homes—grounds Vero’s approach to non-medical care.

We can help families explore care after a hospital stay, during changes in independence, or when a family caregiver needs relief.
