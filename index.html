Who we serve

Support for life’s
changing seasons.

Vero welcomes adults of all ages seeking stability, structure, and compassionate support—including people living on a fixed income, rebuilding after incarceration, navigating mental health recovery, or recovering from health challenges and major life transitions.

Our shared living environment is designed for residents who can live independently day to day, with the comfort of knowing support is only a phone call away.

Meet the founder

Markeisha
Eaves, RN

“Caring for people has always been my purpose.”

Markeisha Eaves has served as a Registered Nurse for more than 17 years. Her experience spans PACU, ICU, Medical-Surgical, Telemetry, Cardiac Catheterization, Pre-Operative Services, Emergency Department support, and other areas of patient care.

For the past 10 years, she has also worked as a travel nurse across more than 30 hospitals, including Level I trauma facilities. That experience—and her belief that everyone deserves dignity, respect, and compassion—grounds the care Vero provides.
